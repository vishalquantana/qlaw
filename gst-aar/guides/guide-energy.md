# GST guide — Energy  

> **DRAFT for departmental review.** Generated from advance rulings held in the corpus. Every statement below is attributed to a ruling; nothing here is a new conclusion of law. Not legal advice.

## Evidence base

- **672 advance rulings** whose sector tags include this sector (a ruling may carry more than one tag)
- 1,320 issue rows, of which **275 (21%) record an outcome**
- 92 distinct substantive provisions cited

**Read the outcome figure carefully.** Only 21% of issues carry a recorded outcome, so this guide is far better at showing *what is disputed* than at showing *how it was decided*.

This gap has been diagnosed rather than assumed. Reading the full orders shows that **95% of the rulings with a missing outcome do state one** — in the form *"In view of the above, we rule as under"* or *"we hold that…"* — so the shortfall is in our extraction, not in the orders. It is a known defect with a known cause, and should not be read as the law being silent on these questions.

Advance rulings bind only the applicant and the officer in that case. They are persuasive, not binding, on anyone else.

## How to read this

- Issues are quoted from the rulings; wording is the applicant's or the Authority's, not ours.
- Where no outcome is recorded, the corpus does not state one. **A blank is left blank deliberately** — a guessed outcome would be worse than none.
- This guide is regenerable: re-run `industry_guide.py` as rulings are added, rather than editing it by hand.
