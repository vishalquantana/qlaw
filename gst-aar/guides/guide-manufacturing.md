# GST guide — Manufacturing  

> **DRAFT for departmental review.** Generated from advance rulings held in the corpus. Every statement below is attributed to a ruling; nothing here is a new conclusion of law. Not legal advice.

## Evidence base

- **1,223 advance rulings** whose sector tags include this sector (a ruling may carry more than one tag)
- 2,320 issue rows, of which **574 (25%) record an outcome**
- 117 distinct substantive provisions cited

**Read the outcome figure carefully.** Only 25% of issues carry a recorded outcome, so this guide is far better at showing *what is disputed* than at showing *how it was decided*.

This gap has been diagnosed rather than assumed. Reading the full orders shows that **95% of the rulings with a missing outcome do state one** — in the form *"In view of the above, we rule as under"* or *"we hold that…"* — so the shortfall is in our extraction, not in the orders. It is a known defect with a known cause, and should not be read as the law being silent on these questions.

Advance rulings bind only the applicant and the officer in that case. They are persuasive, not binding, on anyone else.

### `s.2(68)` — definition of 'services'

*Outcomes across the issues citing this provision: unknown 42, ruling_given 3, withdrawn 2*

- **What is the GST rate on job work services for handicraft brass items (raw brass HSN 7403, statues sold under HSN 8306)? Does the same rate apply to handicraft wood items under HSN 4420?**
  - Held: 5% concessional rate under Entry 26 of Notification 11/2017-CT(R) applicable only while applicant is registered and goods are genuinely handmade.
  - *ruling 2, Rajasthan* — https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/8-order_tushar_agarwal.pdf
- **Whether Resin Statues/Vases are classifiable under HSN 97030090 (Original Sculptures and Statuary)?**
  - Held: No. Resin statues are not original sculptures; they fall under HSN 3926, not HSN 9703.
  - *ruling 2, Rajasthan* — https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/8-order_tushar_agarwal.pdf
- **Whether Deity of marble (Ch. 68) and wooden deity (Ch. 44) are exempt under Entry 124 of Notification 09/2025-CT(R) read with 10/2025-IT(R), and what is the correct 8-digit HSN?**
  - Held: Both deities are exempt under Entry 124 of Notification 10/2025-CT(R). Marble deity falls under Ch. 68 and wooden deity under Ch. 44.
  - *ruling 2, Rajasthan* — https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/8-order_tushar_agarwal.pdf
- **Whether tanker/tipper fabrication on owner chassis is supply of services**
  - *ruling 1059, Gujarat* — https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/23_vasant_fabricators_pvt_ltd_1.pdf

## How to read this

- Issues are quoted from the rulings; wording is the applicant's or the Authority's, not ours.
- Where no outcome is recorded, the corpus does not state one. **A blank is left blank deliberately** — a guessed outcome would be worse than none.
- This guide is regenerable: re-run `industry_guide.py` as rulings are added, rather than editing it by hand.
