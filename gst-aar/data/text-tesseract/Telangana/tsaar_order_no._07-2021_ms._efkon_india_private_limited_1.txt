In the reference 1st cited, M/s. Efkon India Private Limited,   has sought certain 
clarifications in Advance Ruling. Through the reference 2nd cited, Assistant 
Commissioner(ST), Punjagutta Circle, were requested to go through the attachment 
and inform the above pendency of the issue and also requested to offer his/her 
comments on the clarification sought by applicant. 
 
In the reference 3rd cited, a personal hearing notice was given to M/s. Efkon 
India Private Limited, posting their case on 06-08-2021. Heard the case 
 
However, in the reference 4th cited, M/s. Efkon India Private Limited, have filed 
a letter, that the major portion of the activities agreed under contract were duly 
performed by the Company, in other words, the contractual activities are almost 
completed as on date as per the terms and conditions agreed under the contract and 
there is no much further activity to be done now. And hence decided to not to pursue 
the application for clarification and wishes to withdraw the same. 
 
In view, of the above, the application filed by M/s. Efkon India Private Limited, 
for withdrawl is considered and hence the application filed by the applicant may be 
treated as infructuous and dismissed as withdrawn.
