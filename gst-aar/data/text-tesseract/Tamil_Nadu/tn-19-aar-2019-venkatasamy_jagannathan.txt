In the instant case the Profit Sharing Agreement is between various parties who are
all current investors / shareholders of SHA and the applicant. However, the
shareholders are not the company and they cannot and do not act on behalf of the
company. Further, in the email communication to IRDAI by Shri Kannan Unni CM -
