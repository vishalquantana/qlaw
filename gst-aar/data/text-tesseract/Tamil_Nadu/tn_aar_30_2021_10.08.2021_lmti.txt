In the instant case, the courses offered by the applicant namely Mechanic (Motor
Vehicle), Electrician, Sewing Technologr are courses approved by National Council
for Vocational Training (NCVT/Central) which has been evidenced by the certificates

the name of the institute. In respect of Mechanic Refrigeration& Air Condition and

said vocational courses, it is clear that the courses are affiliated to the NCVT/SCVT
which falls uncler S1. No. 66 Notification 12l2O17 Central Tax (Rate) as education as
