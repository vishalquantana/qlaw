8. Whether the activity of the applicant is supply of service or supply of
goods? The activity of construction being done by the applicant involves both
supply of goods and supply of scrviccs. This means that the activity of
construction is a taxable activity and the taxability of it is prescribed under
clause (b) of Rule 5 of the Schedule-II of the Act, which is reproduced below.

5. Supply of services
The following shall be treated as supply of services, namely:-

earlier,

Explanation. -For the purposes of this clause-

(1) the expression “competent authority” means the Government or any

17. In the instant case, the applicant first exccute a Memorandum of
