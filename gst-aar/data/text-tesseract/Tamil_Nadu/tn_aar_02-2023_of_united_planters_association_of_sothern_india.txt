1. Whether the applicant is eligible to avail the benefit of Notification No. 14/2018 -

The applicant is eligible to claim exemption vide serial number 77A of

cases is applicable in our case?

Interpretation of law is not mandated to Advance Ruling Authority under

Section 97(2). Therefore, as discussed in para 6.1 supra, no ruling on the same.
