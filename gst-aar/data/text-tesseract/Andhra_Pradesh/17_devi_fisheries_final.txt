8.4 The point for determlnation here is as to whether the export of speclfied pre-
packaged and labelled frozen Shrimp meant for export would fall within the
meaning of the definition of 'pre-packaged and labellcd commodity' under the
Legal Metrology Act, 2009, and the rules made there under, as defined under
explanation (ii) of the respective Notifications, which is reproduced below :-
"The expression pre-packaged and labelled' means a 'pre-packaged
commodity' as defined in clause (l) sub section (1) of section 2 of the Legal
Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010) where, the package in which the commodity
is pre-packed or a label securely affixed thereto is required to bear the
declarations under the provisions of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of
2010) and the rules made there under."
Further, Section 2(1) of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), defines the
term" pre-packaged commodity" as follows:


"pre-packaged commodity" means a commodity which without the purchaser
being present is placed in a package of whatever nature, whether sealed or
not, so that the product contained therein has a pre-determined quantity,
Therefore, we find that a commodity to be considered as pre-packed and
labelled' shall associate with the following features, viz.,
a. that which comprises a prc-determined quantity as circumscribed under
the meaning of "pre-packaged commodity" vidc Section 2( 1) of the Lcaal
Metrology Act, and
b. that which is required to bear the declarations under the provisions of thc
Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010) and the rules made there under.
