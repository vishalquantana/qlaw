7. Discussion and Findingsl
We have examined the issues raised in the application and the submissions made
by the authorised representative of the applicant as well, regarding the tax liability
of the transaction involved.
As seen from the application, the applicant, M/s. Siddhartha Constructions entered
into a contract with Andhra Pradesh Industrial lnfrastructure Corporation Ltd., (APIIC)
for "providing Power Infrastructure, fire Fighting & alarm System and street lightinq to
the proposed RBF shed No's 1to 4 at YSR-EMC, Y.S.R. Kadapa District".
The issue to be decided is whether the applicant is eligible to avaal the concessional
rate of GST at 12olo as prescribed in Sl.No.3(vi) of the Notification no. 11/2017-Central
Tax (Rate) daled 28.06.2017, as amended from time to time
The Government of India, vide notification No. 11/2017 - Central Tax (Rate),
dated - 28th lune 2017 notified the rate of GST applicable on supply of services.
The said notification has been amended from time to time.
Vide Notification No. 241201,7 - Central Tax (Rate), Dated: 21.09.2017,
Government of India by inserting entry No. (vi) notified,
"Concessional GST rate of 60/0 for the construction services provided to Central
Government, State Government, Union Territory, a local authority or a
Governmental Authority by way of construction, erection, commissioning,
installation, completion, fitting out, repair, maintenance, renovation, or
alteration of -
a) a civil structure or any other original works meant predominantly for use
other than for commerce, industryt or any other business or profession."
This context necessitates the examination of two issues:

b) Consequently whether the construction work taken up by the applicant is
