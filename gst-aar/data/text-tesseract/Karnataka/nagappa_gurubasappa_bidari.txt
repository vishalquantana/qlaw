sae Section 98(2) proviso (i) of the CGST Act 2017 stipulates that “the

application is already pending or decided in any proceedings in the case of an
applicant under any of the provisions of this Act”. In the instant case, admittedly

RULING

The application filed by the Applicant for advance ruling is rejected in terms
of Section 98(2) proviso (i) of the CGST Act 2017, as the proceedings on the
issue have already been initiated.
