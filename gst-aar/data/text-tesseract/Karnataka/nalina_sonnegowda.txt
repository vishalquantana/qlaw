2. The Applicant intends to procure chia seeds (Chia is an edible seed that comes
from the desert plant Salvia Hispanic) from farmers and vendors and supply the same
as food item in wholesale (not for sowing purpose).

start the business due to covid and other circumstances and thus requested to
permit them to withdraw the instant application.

RULING

withdrawn.
