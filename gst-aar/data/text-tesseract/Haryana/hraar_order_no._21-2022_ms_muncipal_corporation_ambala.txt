ORDER

The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under section
97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 12.10.2019. In this matter, notice
for hearing was issued to the applicant. Brief facts of the case are that the applicant has
sought an advance ruling whether exemption from the tax leviability as per notification
no. 12/2017-Central Tax(Rate) dated 28.06.2017 is applicable for the services rendered
by him or not? Whereas, the applicant has informed on 29.11.2022 vide memo no.
MCA/Acctt/2022/2229 dated 29.11.2022 that the competent Authority has decided
that there is no need to continue further in this matter because the same question
have previously been decided by the Authority of Advance Ruling, Chandigarh. So, the
ARA-01 dated 12.10.2019 is being withdrawn.

The authority has considered the matter accordingly. The application
dated 12.10.2019 is being dismissed as withdrawn.
