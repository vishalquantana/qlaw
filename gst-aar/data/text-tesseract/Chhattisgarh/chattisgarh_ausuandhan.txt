The ruling so sought by the Applicant is accordingly answered as under:

Man power services provided by the applicant to the various Central and State

Government departments is liable for GST at the applicable rate and is not

eligible to “Nil” rate of tax provided under sr. no. 3 of the Notification No

12/2017-Central (Rate), dated 28-6-2017 on the grounds put forth by the

applicant being not covered under the functions envisaged under article 243G

and 243W of the Constitution of India. The said service falls under the SAC
i 998513 attracting 18% GST (9% CGST and 9% CGGST)
