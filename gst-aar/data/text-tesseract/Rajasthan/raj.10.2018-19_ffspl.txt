1. SUBMISSION OF THE APPLICANT

M/s Forbes Facility Services Private Limited is a company registered under
GST engaged in business/activities of providing catering services to various segments
of Business Houses, including Corporate and Universities.

The applicant is a services provider company i.e. it prepares food at recipient
premises or within the office in some cases using their own manpower by using
infrastructure such as electricity, water and cooking gas provided by the establishment.
Payment of supplies is received from the company. The applicant submitted that they

normally charge GST @ 18% classifying their services under heading 996333 as

outdoor catering.
2. ISSUE TO BE DECIDED

Whether clarification F.No 354/03/2018 dt.08.01.20180 is also applicable to
“Industrial Canteen”?
3. PROCEEDINGS

In the matter personal hearing was fixed for 28.7.2018 but no one appeared on
the stipulated date from applicant side and vide their letter, which was received on
06.08.2018 informed about withdrawal of their application and requested to drop the

verification and examination of the said Application.

RULING

The application is dismissed having been withdrawn by the applicant. Ordered

accordingly.
