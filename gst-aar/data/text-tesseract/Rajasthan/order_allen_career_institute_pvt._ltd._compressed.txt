2.45 It is respectfully submitted that Section 12 of the IGST Act is a self-

SF are a identifiable location, consistent with the legislative scheme

be addressed by Section 12(5) of IGST Act. Consequently, treating

amount to importing virtual or fictional performance into a

provision that is otherwise grounded in physical reality, leading to
