be available for ITC?

Q1.2. Whether GST paid on labour supply for carrying out repair of building shall be
available for ITC, where material and supervision is provided by the applicant?

Q1.3. Will it make any difference if aforementioned works are carried out in a composite

composite supply under works contract?
