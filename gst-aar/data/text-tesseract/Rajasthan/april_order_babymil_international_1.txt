11) On going through this chapter (chapter 19), we find that it deals with Preparations of
cereals, flour, starch or milk; pastry cooks’ product.

by the applicant, we find that under chapter 19 it is described that milk product is one
of the constituent of the final product whereas Chapter 4 deals with the milk product
only. The Principle/dominant item of the applicant is manufacturing of infant milk
formula containing cereals, protein supplement etc. which is a substitute to mother’s
milk for the infants. Therefore, it is more appropriate to classify the manufactured
product by the applicant under HSN 19011090.
