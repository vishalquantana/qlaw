The present application has been filed under section 97 of the Central Goods and Services

Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to

as “the CGST Act and MGST Act” respectively] M/s. Mukand Limited, the applicant is seeking
an advance ruling in respect of the following questions.
a. Whether GST is applicable on recovery of nominal amount for availing the facility
of Canteen at the factory premises when it is not a supply as per clause 1 of

Schedule III of CGST Act;
b. Whether ITC is available on GST charged for the provision of Canteen services

for the employees at the factory premises?

c. Whether GST is applicable on payment of notice pay by an employee to the
Applicant-employer in lieu of notice period, under clause 5(e) of Schedule II of
CGST Act.

d. Whether GST is applicable on the amount of premium of Medical Insurance
Policy of for the employees and their dependents at actuals covered under the HR
Policy;

e, Whether GST is applicable on recovery of nominal amount for availing the facility
of transportation to and from the factory premises when it is not a supply;

f. Whether Input Tax Credit is available on GST charged for transportation facility
provided to the employees of the Applicant-Company;

g. Whether Input Tax Credit is available on GST charged for gardening expenses of
the Applicant-Company.

The applicant, via email dated 16.03.2026, submitted that after internal discussions with
the management, they have decided not to pursue the matter and have expressed their
intention to voluntarily withdraw the subject application filed on 20.05.2022.

The request of the applicant to withdraw their application voluntarily and
unconditionally is hereby allowed, without going into the merits or detailed facts of the
case.
