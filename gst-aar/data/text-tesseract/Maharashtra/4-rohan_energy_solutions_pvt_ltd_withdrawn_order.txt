PROCEEDINGS

(Under Section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods
and Services Tax Act, 2017)
The present application has been filed under section 97 of the Central Goods and Services

Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to
as “the CGST Act and MGST Act” respectively] M/s. Rohan Energy Solutions Pvt Ltd, the
applicant is seeking an advance ruling in respect of the following questions.

1, What GST rate is applicable for the product Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)?

2. What will be the HS N Code for the product Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF)?

The applicant, vide email dated 07.05.2026, stated that they do not wish to seek any
clarification on the question raised and have expressed their intention to voluntarily
withdraw the same. Accordingly, they have requested permission to withdraw the subject

application filed on 03.09.2020.
