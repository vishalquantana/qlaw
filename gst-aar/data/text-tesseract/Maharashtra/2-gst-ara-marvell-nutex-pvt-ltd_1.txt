(Under Section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017)
The present application has been filed under section 97 of the Central Goods and

Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
[hereinafter referred to as “the CGST Act and MGST Act” respectively]
M/s. Marvell Nutex pvt ltd, where the applicant is seeking an advance ruling in
respect of the following questions.
Q1. Whether activity relating to Sale/ Transfer of leasehold land & Building and
also to obtain permission for such sale would be taxable?
