Under the MGST Act, the deeming fiction or legal fiction has been created whereby the
supply made by a taxable person to related person (section 15) or distinct person
(section 25) for himself (schedule 1) is liable to be treated as *supply* made by one
person to another. Even if such supply of goods or services is not reciprocated by
consideration by the recipient, still such activity is liable to be treated as supply under
MGST Act.
