Act, 2017 defines the expression ‘business’ under section 2(17) of the CGST

is as under: -
"(17) "business" includes -
(a) any trade, commerce, manufacture, profession, vocation, adventure, wager or any

This is an inclusive definition wherein various aspects have been listed in

these activities or any other similar activities. The last phrase ‘whether or not it is

Cambridge Dictionary - less important than the thing something is connected

Dictionary.com - happening or likely to happen in an unplanned or

"The transportation of employees from distance of about 40 kms for reaching factory is
