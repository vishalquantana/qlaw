As per section 17(5) (c) of the CGST Act, 2017, input tax credit shall not be
available in respect of the works contract services when supplied for
construction of an immovable property (other than plant and machinery)
except where it is an input service for further supply of works contract service.

of business and is using such services received for further supply of works
contract service. When a works contract is for the construction of plant and

machinery, the ITC of the tax paid to the works contractor would be available

to the recipient, whatever is the business of the recipient. This is because works
ontract in respect of plant and machinery comes within the exclusion clause
f the negative list and ITC would be available when used in the course or
furtherance of business.
