Question 1:- If the tax rate of M/s. Mahalaxmi B T Patil Honai Constructions JV (Referred to as JV) is
NIL as per SI No 3A- Chapter No. 9954 as per Notification No. 12/2017 C.T. (Rate) dated

Chapter No. 9954 as per Notification No. 01/2018- C.T. (Rate) dated 25.01.2018
(amendments in the Notification No 11/2017- C.T. (Rate) dated 18.06.2017)?

Answer:- Answered in the affirmative BUT ONLY TILL 31.12.2021.
