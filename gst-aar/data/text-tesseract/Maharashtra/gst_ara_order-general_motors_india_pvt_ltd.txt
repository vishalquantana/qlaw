5.11.15 When a lessor leases a parcel of land to a lessee with the permission to
construct buildings, the legal ownership of the land remains with the lessor,
and the lessee acquires only possessory rights over the land as per the terms
of the lease agreement. The lessee may enjoy the right to use and occupy the
buildings during the lease term but does not have the authority to transfer
ownership of the buildings separately from the land. The lessee may, subject
to the lease agreement, remove movable fixtures and certain attachments to
the land before the lease expires, provided such removal does not cause
damage to the property. Under the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, section
54, a sale is defined as the transfer of ownership in exchange for a price paid
or promised, or part-paid and part-promised. As the lessee does not have
ownership rights over the plot of land and the buildings over it, no sale of

immovable property can take place.
