RAEKKKAK KEK

The applicant in its application for rectifying an Order No. 22 for Advance Ruling issued
by Authority for Advance Ruling on 25.01.2019, submitted that in online application, name of the
company and GSTIN were correctly filled but while submitting the hard copy of application form
GST ARA-01 name of the company and GSTIN were in advertently submitted Rudhrabhishek
Enterprises Limited and GSTIN 09AAGCRI735AIZP instead of Rudrabhishek Enterprises
Limited and GSTIN 09AAACRO707L2ZD.

Therefore, as per the request of applicant, exercising the power vested in the Authority U/s
102 of the CGST Act, 2017, a rectification order is issued as under:-

“Rudhrabhishek Enterprises Limited” and GSTIN “O9AAGCRI735A1ZP”,
i) In the subject mentioned at page 01 of the order no. 22.

ii) In paragraph 01 at page no. 01 of the order no. 22.

iii) | At page no. 08 of the order no. 22.

will be read as “Rudrabhishek Enterprises Limited” and GSTIN “09AAACRO707L2ZD”.
