repair and maintenance of any immovable property. In the instant case the O&M
services are meant for maintenance of the ZLD plant, an immovable property and
hence the O & M services are covered under Sr. No. 3(iii) of Notification No.
11/2017-Central Tax (Rate) dated June 28, 2017 as amended and attracts GST @
12%.

In view of the foregoing, we pass the following
RULING
The service of supply, erection, commissioning and installation of waste-
water pretreatment plant (ZLD plant) and the services of Operation and
Maintenance (O&M) of the said plant together is composite supply of works
contract classified under SAC 9954 and is liable to CGST @ 6% and KGST
@6% in terms of entry No.3(iii) of the Notification No. 11/2017-Central Tax
(Rate) dated 28.06.2017 as amended by Notification No. 20/2017-Central
Tax (Rate) dated 22.08.2017 and Notification No. 31/2017-Central Tax
(Rate) dated 13.10.2017.
