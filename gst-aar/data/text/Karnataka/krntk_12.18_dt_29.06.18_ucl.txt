2. 
The Applicant is engaged in the manufacture and supply of cement 
and clinker & supplies the said goods to the authorized dealers / stockists, 
under the cover of tax invoice after discharging the applicable GST and the 
said dealers supply the same in the regional markets to the ultimate 
customers.  At times due to dynamic market conditions the dealers sell the 
goods at the price lower than their cost price and in such cases the 
applicant pays certain amount as „rate difference‟ commonly known as 
„Trade Discount‟. 
 
3. 
In view of the above, the Applicant has sought for Advance Ruling on 
the question that “Whether the amount paid to dealer towards „rate 
difference‟ post supply can be considered for the purpose of arriving at the 
„transaction value‟ in terms of Section 15 of the Central Goods and Service 
Act‟2017 ?”.  But the Applicant requested to permit them to withdraw the 
application filed for advance ruling vide their letter dated 29.05.2018, 
even prior to personal hearing. 
 
4. 
In view of the foregoing, we pass the following 
 
R U L I N G 
 
The application filed by the Applicant for advance ruling is dismissed 
as withdrawn.
