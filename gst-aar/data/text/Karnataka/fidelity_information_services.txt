RULING
i. GST is not applicable on recovery of joining bonus and retention bonus on
account of employee's inability to serve the organization (or a particular
department, in case of retention bonus) for a pre-agreed period.
ii. GST is not applicable on recovery of work from home one-time setup
allowance paid to employees in case where the employees exit before
serving the pre-defined period from the payout date.
