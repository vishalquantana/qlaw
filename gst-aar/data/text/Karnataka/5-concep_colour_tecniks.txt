M/s Concept Colour Tecniks Pvt. Ltd, (hereinafter referred to as the
"Applicant"), having GSTIN 29AABCC6549A1ZT and principal place of business at #
No. C 97, KSSIDC Industrial Estate, Bommasandra Hosur Road, Bengaluru Urban,
Karnataka, 560099, filed an application for Advance Ruling under Section 97 of the
Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 read with Rule 104 of the Central Goods
and Services Tax Rules, 2017 and the corresponding provisions of the Karnataka
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, in FORM GST ARA-01.
It was observed that, in terms of Section 97(1) of the CGST/KGST Act, 2017 read
with Rule 104 of the CGST/KGST Rules, 2017, an application for Advance Ruling is
required to be accompanied by a fee of 25,000/- each under the CGST Act and the
KGST Act. However, the Applicant had not discharged the prescribed fee in full and
Soily For accordingly advised to rectify the defect by remitting the requisite balance fee.
The Applicant is engaged in the business of powder coating and zinc phosphating
(chemical conversion coating) for industrial and automative components. Further,
