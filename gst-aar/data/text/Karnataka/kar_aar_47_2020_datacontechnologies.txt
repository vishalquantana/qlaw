6.5 It is observed, from the letter dated 06.12.2018 of BSEB, submitted by the
applicant containing reference of work order dated 10.11.2018, that the applicant
was given the job to scan the OMR Flying slip, OMR marks Foil with barcode
sticker, scanning of OMR attendance sheet and scanning OMR Absentee sheet
along with data extraction and finalization of data in all the four categories.

6.6 Now we proceed to examine whether the aforesaid work, allotted to the
applicant by the BSEB, covered under the conduction of examination or not.
It is an undisputed fact that the process of conducting examination is not limited/
restricted to a test centre. Examination is an incomplete activity without
assessment. Scanning of answer sheets and quantifying marks is an essential part
albeit main objective of the examination process. Educational institutions or the
examinees do not look at these activities in isolation. We therefore observe that the

stated activity of the applicant is exempted by virtue of Sr. No.66 of Notification No.
12/2017-CT (R) dated 28.06.2017.
