3. When by application of rule 2(b) or for any other reason, goods are, prima facie, classifiable under

(a) The heading which provides the most specific description shall be preferred to headings providing

As is already stated, the applicant imports the goods. During the
importation, the goods are classified by Customs under the Customs Tariff Act,

On the aforementioned background, we find that the applicant is
