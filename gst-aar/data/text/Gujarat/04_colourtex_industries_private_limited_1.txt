BRIEF FACTS
M/s. Colourtex Industries Private Limited, hereinafter referred to as Colourtex for the
sake of brevity, supplies Tamarind Kernal Powder (TKP). Colourtex, in Central Excise
regime, classified TKP at CETH 1302, as per the CBIC Circular No. 1037/25/2016-CX
dated 19% July 2016 vide which clarified that the product TKP shall be classified under
tariff item 1302 32 90 of CETA, 1985 as a product derived from the seed of the tamarind
fruit. The said classification shall apply to both treated (modified) tamarind kernel
powder and plain (unmodified) tamarind kernel powder.
2. Colourtex submits that there is no dispute that TKP is classifiable under Chapter 13 of

3. Colourtex submits that Tariff Item No.1302 32 90, which is for Mucilages and
thickeners, whether or not modified, derived from locust beans, locust Bean seeds or guar
seeds. Colourtex submits that TKP is not derived from locust beans / locust beans seeds/
guar seeds. TKP is manufactured by grinding Tamarind Kernel Seeds derived from
tamarind tree. Hence, the same is classifiable under Tariff Item No. 1302 39 00, i.e., the
category of 'Others'.
4. Question on which Advance Ruling sought

2.3 of the annexure attached.
