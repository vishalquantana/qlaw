37. Considering the fact that Nirma University has been set up by a passing

Parliament.
38.
As discussed earlier, usage of word 'or' between (i) and (ii) coupled with
the fact that clause (i) is followed by "," unlike the clause (ii) is followed by
means that the condition of fifty-one per cent. or more participation by way of
equity or control, to carry out any function, would be applicable to the body
which is established by the Government. This means that the board/ body set
up by an Act of Parliament or State legislature is independent and is not bound
by above condition.
39. However, recently, a circular is issued vide reference No. 76/50/2018-
GST dated 31st December, 2018 wherein the clarity is provided on similar
aspect or legal interpretation. Accordingly, to Circular, it has been clarified that
the long line written in clause (a) in Notification No.50/2018-Central Tax dated
13.09.2018 is applicable to both the items (i) and (ii) of clause (a) of the said
Notification.
40. In other words, the provisions of Section 51 of the CGST Act, are
applicable only to such authority or a board or any other body set up by an Act
of parliament or a State legislature or established by any Government in which
fifty-one per cent. or more participation by way of equity or control, is with the
Government.
