FINDINGS:
Revenue has neither submitted its comments nor appeared for hearing.
7. We have considered the submissions made by the Applicant in their application for
advance ruling as well as the submissions made by authorised signatory, during the
personal hearing proceedings on 26-9-22 before this authority. We also considered the
issue involved, on which advance ruling is sought by the applicant, relevant facts & the
applicant's interpretation of law.
8. At the outset we would like to make it clear that the provisions of CGST Act and
GGST Act are in parimateria and have the same provisions in like matter and differ from
each other only on a few specific provisions. Therefore, unless a mention is particularly
made to such dissimilar provisions, a reference to the CGST Act would also mean
