9. The term "business" is defined in Section 2(17) of the GSTAct, which reads
like this:-
"business" includes:- (a) any trade, commerce, manufacture, profession, vocation, adventure,

(b) any activity or transaction in connection with or incidents! or ancillary to sub-clause (a); ...
From the plane reading of the definition
