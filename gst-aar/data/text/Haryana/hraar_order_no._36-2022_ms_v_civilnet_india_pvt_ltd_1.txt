The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under
section 97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 11.03.2020. In this
matter, notice for hearing on the ARAO] was issued to the applicant on 16.09.2022
for 18.10.2022 but none appeared on behalf of the applicant on the said date.
Further, a notice for hearing was issued to the applicant on 20.10.2022 for
15.12.2022.In response to which the applicant has send a letter and he has stated as
that the advance ruling was filed by us through our consultants with whom we are
not in association as on date. Further, the copy of the advance ruling and all other
related documents are lying with them only and we don't have any such records
with us. Thus, we lost the track of any advance ruling as filed by consultants, hence
they are not in position to provide the same and even we don't have any such
pending transaction or question in our memory to be decided by the Hon'ble
Authority for Advance Ruling. Further, the question as placed in the advance
ruling is also generic and we don’t have any such prospective transaction as on
date which require answers in the advance ruling. Further, since the application of
advance ruling is almost 2 years old in itself. , there are likely chances that the
transaction must have been either dropped to be undertaken or may be undertaken.
The applications for advance ruling become infructous and it is irrelevant to
participate in the proceedings as on date. We hereby request your goodself to take
the above facts on record and treat the advance ruling as not ever filed in absence
of records and facts with us. Thus, the application for advance ruling should be
