ORDER
The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under section
97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 14.11.2019. In this matter, notice
for hearing was issued to the applicant. Brief facts of the case are that applicant is the
authorized dealer of Tata Motors dealing in commercial vehicle segment. He also
provide special purpose vehicles with software GPS as required by clients. He
purchases readymade trucks from Tata Motors and on these trucks a special purpose
body is mounted/fitted for collecting garbage and used as Garbage Collection Vehicle.
The final product i.e. garbage collection vehicles are used for loading or uploading of
garbage. The applicant has raised query in this case as
1. Whether these special purpose vehicle will fall under the chapter heading 8705
or 8704?
In this regard, the applicant has informed on 10.10.2022 that he does not
want to pursue the issue. And, he has submitted the withdrawal application. The
authority has considered the matter accordingly. The advance ruling application dated
14.11.2019 is being dismissed as withdrawn.
