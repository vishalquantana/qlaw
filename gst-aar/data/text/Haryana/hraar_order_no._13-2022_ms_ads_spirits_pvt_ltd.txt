The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under
section 97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 21.06.2021 whereas
manual copy of the application has been submitted before the authority on
07.10.2022. In this matter, notice for hearing on the ARA-01 was issued to the
applicant on 16.09.2022 for 18.10.2022. Further, the applicant was given three
more opportunities to present the case in person. Despite the fact that the applicant
has been provided four opportunities to present the case before the authority, he
has not responded. A final notice for hearing was issued to the applicant on
16.12.2022 for 11.01.2023 but to no avail. In the light of fact that the applicant has
been given sufficient opportunities to present the matter, it can be said that he is
not interested to seek the advance ruling. So, the application dated 21.06.2021 is
dismissed as infructous.
