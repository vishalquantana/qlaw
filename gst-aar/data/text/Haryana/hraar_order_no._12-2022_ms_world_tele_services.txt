The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under
section 97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 17.08.2021. In this
matter, notice for hearing on the ARA-01 was issued to the applicant on
16.09.2022 for 18.10.2022. Further, the applicant was given four more
opportunities to present the case in person. Despite the fact that the applicant has
been provided five opportunities to present the case before the authority, he has not
responded. A final notice for hearing was issued to the applicant on 16.12.2022 for
11.01.2023 but to no avail. Besides the applicable fee as per the provision of the
Act has not been paid by him. In the light of fact that the applicant is not
responding and sufficient opportunities have been given to him, the application
dated 17.08.2021 is dismissed as infructous.
