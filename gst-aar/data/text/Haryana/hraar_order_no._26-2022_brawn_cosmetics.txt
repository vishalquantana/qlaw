The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under
section 97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 14.07.2020 whereas
no manual copy of the application has been submitted before the authority by him.
In this matter, notice for hearing on the ARAO1 was issued to the applicant on
16.09.2022 for 18.10.2022 but none appeared on behalf of the applicant on the said
date. Further, the applicant was given two more opportunities to present the case in
person. Despite the fact that the applicant has been provided three opportunities,
there has been no communication from the applicant on the issue. A final notice for
hearing was issued to the applicant for 23.12.2022 to present the matter in person
but to no avail. In the light of facts that the applicant is not responding and

sufficient opportunities have been given to him, the application dated 14.07.2020 is

dismissed as infructous. In case, the applicant still needs an advance ruling on the

matter he can file the application afresh in accordance with the provision of the

CGST/HGST Act, 2017.
