The applicant has submitted an advance ruling application under
section 97 of CGST/HGST Act, 2017 through online mode on 22.04.2019 whereas
no manual copy of the application has been submitted before the authority by him.
In this matter, notice for hearing on the ARA01 was issued to the applicant on
07.09.2022 for 28.09.2022 but none appeared on behalf of the applicant on the said
date. Further, the applicant was given three more opportunities to present the case
in person. Despite the fact that the applicant has been provided four opportunities,
there has been no communication from the applicant on the issue. A final notice for
hearing was issued to the applicant for 23.12.2022 but to no avail. In the light of
facts that the applicant is not responding and sufficient opportunities have been
given to him, the application dated 22.04.2019 is dismissed and infructous. In case,
the applicant still needs an advance ruling on the matter he can file the application
afresh in accordance with the provision of the CGST/HGST Act, 2017.
