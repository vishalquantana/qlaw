b) Now we examine whether the construction work in which the applicant is engaged

The applicant claims that the works involved in the contract are used for the
offices of the Hon'ble Minister for Industries and not for the business interests or
transactions of APIIC. The point under discussion is whether the work involved,
i,e, "providing int€rior works, furniture and internal electrification of the office space of
