Intermedaary under Goods and Services Tax:

means "a broker, an agent or any other persont by whatever name called, who
arranges or facilitates the supply of goods or services or both, or securities,
between two or more persons, but does not include a person who supplies such
goods or services or both or securities on his own account'"
In terms of provisions of Section 2(5) of the CGST Act, 2Ol7;
"'Agent'means a persont including a factor, broker, commission agent, arhatia,
