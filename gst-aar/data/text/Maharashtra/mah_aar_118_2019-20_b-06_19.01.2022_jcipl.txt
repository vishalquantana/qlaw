reconstruct anatomy or function of body affected due to congenital defects, development
abnormalities, injury or trauma. The applicant has not made any submissions to show that
Bleaching of Teeth is undertaken to restore or to reconstruct anatomy or function of body
affected due to congenital defects, development abnormalities, injury or trauma. In fact, from
the applicant’s submissions it is clearly seen that Bleaching of Teeth is a pure cosmetic surgery
used to enhance the look of the Teeth and therefore we hold that the said service is not covered
under SAC 999312. In fact the said service is explicitly covered under SAC 999722 (Cosmetic
treatment (including cosmetic/plastic surgery), manicuring and pedicuring services) which
includes:-

(i) face and beauty treatment, including cosmetic treatment/surgeries.
