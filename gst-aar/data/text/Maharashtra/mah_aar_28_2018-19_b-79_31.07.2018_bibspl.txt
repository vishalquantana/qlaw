PROCEEDINGS

(under section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017)

The present application has been filed under section 97 of the Central Goods and Services
Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to as
“the CGST Act and MGST Act”] by Bauli India Bakes and Sweets Private Limited, the

applicant, seeking an advance ruling in respect of the following question :

Whether it is admissible to carry forward in GST Transition the un-availed CENVAT
credit duties paid on capital goods received in the factory prior to 01 July 2017 and which are

to be used in manufacturing activities ?

The preliminary hearing in the matter was held on 03.07.2018 and the Final hearing was
fixed for 25.07.2018 , but applicant has filed letter dated 24.07.2018 received on 24.07.2018 in this

office with request to allow withdrawal of the application filed on 23.05.2018.

The request of the applicant to withdraw the application voluntarily and unconditionally

llowed without going into the merits or detailed facts of this advance ruling application
