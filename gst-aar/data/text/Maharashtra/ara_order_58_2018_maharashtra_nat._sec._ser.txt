In the subject case the applicant is providing pure services (without the supply of goods), as
submitted by them, to PCMC. We find that the said services are in relation to any functions entrusted to a
Municipality under article 243W of the Constitution. The agreement between the applicant and PCMC very

Providing assistance to the Security Guards of PCMC is an activity in relation to various functions as
enumerated above which have been entrusted to a Municipality under article 243W of the Constitution.
Hence we find that the applicant is entitled to the benefit of Notification No. 12/2007-CT(Rate) dated
28.06.2017.
