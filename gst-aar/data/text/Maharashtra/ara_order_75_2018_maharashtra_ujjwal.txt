will have to discharge their GST liability at a rate which will be the tariff rate for the goods, in this case
LEDs and fixtures. To answer the question of the applicant, we need to find the Tariff classification of
LEDs/fixtures. We find that ‘LED Lights or Fixtures including LED Lamps’ are covered under the Sub-
Heading 9405 40 90 of the GST Tariff, 2017 which are taxable @ 12% (6% each of CGST and SGST).
