purchasing goods from M/s Innospec on the basis of purchase orders received from their

We find that proviso to Section 5(1) of the IGST Act states that “Provided that the
integrated tax on goods imported into India shall be levied and collected in accordance with the

Thus from the above it is clear that integrated tax on goods imported into India is to be
levied and collected in accordance with Section 3 of the Customs Tariff Act, 1975 and Section 12

frontier after compliance of applicable procedures and payment of duty as applicable.

Thus as per Section 7(2) of the IGST Act and proviso to Section 5(1) of the IGST Act it is
very clear that in respect of import goods there is no levy and collection except in accordance
