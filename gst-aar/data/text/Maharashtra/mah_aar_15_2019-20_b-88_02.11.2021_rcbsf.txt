PROCEEDINGS
(Under Section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and
Services Tax Act, 2017)

The present application has been filed under Section 97 of the Central Goods and Services Tax

Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to as “the CGST

Act and MGST Act” respectively ] by M/s. ROTARY CLUB OF BOMBAY SEA FACE, the applicant, seeking
an advance ruling in respect of the following questions.
Whether the amount collected as membership subscription and admission fees from members is

liable to GST as supply of services?

hereby allowed, without going into the merits or detailed facts of the case.
