The present application has been filed under section 97 of the Central Goods and Services Tax
Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to as “the CGST
Act and MGST Act” respectively | by M/s. Rotary Club of Bombay Peninsula , the applicant, seeking

an advance ruling in respect of the following questions.

1. Whether the amount collected as membership subscription and admission fees from

members is liable to GST as supply of services?
2. If the above receipts are liable to GST can the Club claim Input tax credit of the tax paid

on Banquet and catering services for holding members meetings and various events?
