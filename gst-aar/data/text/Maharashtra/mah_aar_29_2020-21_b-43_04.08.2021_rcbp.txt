PROCEEDINGS

(Under Section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra
Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017)

The present application has been filed under Section 97 of the Central Goods and Services
Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred
to as “the CGST Act and MGST Act” respectively] by M/s. Rotary Club of Bombay Pier

the applicant, seeking an advance ruling in respect of the following questions.

1. Whether the amount collected as membership subscription and admission fees

from members is liable to GST as supply of services?

2. If the above receipts are liable to GST can the Club claim Input tax credit of the
tax paid on Banquet and catering services for holding members meetings and

various events?

The applicant has submitted a letter dated 26.07.2021 and requested that they may be
allowed to voluntarily withdraw their subject application filed on 12.09.2020.
The request of the applicant to withdraw the application voluntarily and unconditionally

is hereby allowed, without going into the merits or detailed facts of the case.
