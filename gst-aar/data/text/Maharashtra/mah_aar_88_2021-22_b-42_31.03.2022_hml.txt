PROCEEDINGS

(Under Section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and
Services Tax Act, 2017)

The present application has been filed under Section 97 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017
and the Maharashtra Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 [hereinafter referred to as “the CGST Act and
MGST Act” respectively ] by M/s. HINDOOSTAN MILLS LIMITED, the applicant, seeking an advance ruling

in respect of the following question.

Whether Goods and Service Tax will be applicable on the consideration and the rate of GST.

Applicant filed online application on system and Advance Ruling office requested via email on
04.03.2022 for hard copy of their application in four sets. The applicant has submitted a letter via email
dated 31.03.2022 and requested that they may be allowed to voluntarily withdraw their subject
application filed on 31.03.2022.

The request of the applicant to withdraw their application voluntarily and unconditionally is
hereby allowed, without going into the merits or detailed facts of the case.

ORDER
(Under section 98 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Maharashtra Goods and
Services Tax Act, 2017)

The Application in GST ARA Form No. 01 of M/s. HINDOOSTAN MILLS LIMITED, vide reference ARA No.
88 Dated 31.03.2022 is disposed of, as being withdrawn voluntarily and unconditionally.
