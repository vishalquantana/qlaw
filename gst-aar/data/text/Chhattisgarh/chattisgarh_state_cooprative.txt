The matter has been examined. Sr. No. 55 o/ Notification 12/2017.
Central Tax (Rate) exempts carrying out an intermediate production process as

market, Job work has been defined under section 2 168) of the CGST Act to
mean any reatment or process undertaken by a person on goods belonging to
another registered person. Further, under Schedule Il (para 3) of the CGST Act
any treatment or process which is applied to anoner person's goods is a supply
of service.
3.
Milling of paddy is not on intermediate production process in relation to
cultivation of plants. It is a process carried out after the process of culfivation is
over and paddy has been harvested. Further, processing of paddy into rice is
not usually carried out by culfivators but by rice millers. Milling of paddy into rice
aiso changes its essential characteristics. Therefore, milling of paddy into rice
cannot be considered as an intermediate production process in relation to
cultivation of plants for food, fibre or other similar products or agricultural
croduce.
in view of the above, it is clarified that miling of paddy into rice is not
eligiole for exemption under S. No. 55 of Notification 12/2017 - Central Tax
/Rate| dared 28" June 2017 and corresponding notifications issued under GST
and UTGST Acts.
5.
GST rate on services by way of job work in relation to all food and food
products falling under Chapters I lo 22 has been reduced from 18% to5% vide
notification No. 31/2017-CT/P) /notification No. 11/2017-CT /Rotel dated 28.6. 17.
S.No. 26 refers). Therefore, it is hereby clarified that milling of paddy into rice on
lob work basis, is liable to GST of the rate of 5%, on the processing charges and
