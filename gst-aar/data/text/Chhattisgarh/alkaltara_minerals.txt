ii. In applicants view, the literal interpretation of GST Notification No. 02/2018-

coal washery but if should be seen as anyone dealing in the same process

ii. Thus, in applicants’ view the coal waste sold by Power Plant should also be not

iv. If cess is applicable on rejected coal again then it tantamount to double

double taxation on goods.
