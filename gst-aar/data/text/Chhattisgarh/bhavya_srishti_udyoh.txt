of ‘ejusdem generis’ for interpretation of th
agriculture...”. The 'ejusdem generis’ principle specifies that “general terms following

particular expressions take their colour and meaning as that of the preceding
expressions”. Applying this, we find that in Chapter 8201, the specifics are spades,
shovels, mattocks, picks, hoes, forks and rakes, axes, bill nooks and similar hewing tools;
secateurs and pruners of any kind; scythes, sickles, hay knives, hedge shears, timber
wedges and the subsequent phrase “and other tools of a kind used in agriculture...” is a
general expression following the specific enumeration. The specifics mentioned are all
of which has a working edge as required for @ tool fo be classified under Chapter 82.
Thus only those hand tools which are of base metal and has a working edge, used as a
hand tool in agriculture merits classification under this residual entry. The product at
hand is definitely not made of base metal nor it has a working edge as required of a
tool under this Chapter and therefore the applicant's product by no stretch of
imagination could be classified under Chapter 8201, as claimed by the applicant.
Classification under a Heading is to be governed only by ihe relevant Tariff-entries,
Heading-description, etc. Heading 8201, does not envisage that all items used in an
agriculture field would be covered therein. The instant product can also not be
Classified under 8201 as Hand tools, such as spades, shovels, maitocks, picks, hoes, forks
and rakes: axes, bill hooks and similar hewing tools; secateurs and pruners of any kind;
scythes, sickles, hay knives, hedge shears, timber wedges and other tools of a kind used
in agriculture, horticulture or forestry. We find no rationale in the opinion put forth by
the applicant in as much as, assuming but not acceding if the said interpretation of
applicant is considered then even the wire fence used for a boundary in agricultural

product is purely a support made of bamboo for creeper vegetables/ crops. Thus we
come to the conclusion that the applicant is not eligible for exemption from tax on the
