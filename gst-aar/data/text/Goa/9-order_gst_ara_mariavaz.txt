As per the supplementary notes to chapter 09 of HSN Classification
1) Heading 0910 includes spices.
2) “Spice” means a group of vegetable products (including seeds, etc.), rich
in essential oils and aromatic principles, and which, because of their taste,
are mainly used as condiments. These products may be whole or in crushed

or powdered form.

Page 6 of 8

3) The addition of other substances to spices shall not affect their inclusion in
spices provided the resulting mixtures retain the essential character of

spices and spices also include products commonly known as “masalas”.

The applicant has also placed on record the printed labels of their 5 products
which makes it clear that all these five products are the mixture of various spices
containing water and preservatives to make them in the form of a paste. It is noted
that there are no ingredients like grinded coconut paste or such other ingredients

so as to make it a ready to eat formulation.

It is seen that each of these five different pastes are required to be applied on the
chicken or fish and marinated for some time and then to be cooked to make it

eatable.

From the list of ingredients provided by the applicant it is clear that the product
includes mixture of various spices with less than 1% in preservatives. Therefore,
it can be safely concluded that the classification of the product falls under chapter
09 of the HSN codes.

As per Notification no. 01/2017 Center Tax (Rate) dated 28/06/2017, it is clear

that the rate of tax applicable on Goods under Chapter 09 of HSN codes are
taxable at 5%.

In the instant case, the rate applicable is identified at sr. no. 44 of Schedule I of
said Notification No. 1/2017 central tax (Rate) dated 28/06/2017. Thus, the HSN

code assignable to the products under question is ‘0910’.
