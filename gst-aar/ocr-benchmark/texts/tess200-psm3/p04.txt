AS per Section 31(2) of CGST Act read with Rule 46 of CGST Rules, a registered
person supplying taxable services shall, before or after the provision of service but
within a prescribed period, issue a tax invoice, showing the description, value,
GSTIN of the recipient, tax charged thereon and such other particulars as may be
prescribed

shall comply with E-invoice provision by obtaining an Invoice Reference Number

Services Tax Electronic Portal and capture the IRN and OR code on the body of the
inwoice.
